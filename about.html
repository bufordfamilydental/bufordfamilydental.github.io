About Buford Family Dental

At Buford Family Dental, we believe that a healthy, vibrant smile is the foundation of lifelong confidence and wellness. Located in the heart of Buford, Georgia, our modern dental practice is designed to provide patients of all ages with first-class care in a relaxed, welcoming environment. Whether you are bringing your child in for their very first checkup or seeking advanced restorative care for yourself, our practice is equipped to meet your family’s unique oral health needs with skill, kindness, and precision.

Modern Dentistry Built Around Your Needs

Dental care should never feel intimidating or transactional. That is why we combine modern dental technology with a deeply personalized clinical approach. From digital diagnostics to advanced treatment techniques, our state-of-the-art facility enables us to perform procedures faster, more accurately, and with greater comfort than ever before. We also believe that exceptional clinical results go hand-in-hand with clear communication. From the moment you step into our office, we take the time to walk you through your treatment plan, explain every recommendation, and discuss all associated costs upfront. There are no hidden fees or unexpected surprises—just straightforward, honest care that puts you in control of your health decisions. To make high-quality dentistry accessible to everyone, we offer flexible financial options tailored to fit a wide variety of household budgets.

Meet Our Experienced Doctor Team

The strength of Buford Family Dental lies in the collaborative expertise and genuine dedication of our dental team. Led by Dr. Ravi Patel alongside Dr. Sun Kim and Dr. Rohit Mukhi, our doctors bring a wealth of knowledge and clinical skills to the practice. Both Dr. Kim and Dr. Mukhi proudly earned their undergraduate degrees from the University of Georgia before completing their dental doctorates at the esteemed Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University. Together with Dr. Patel, our doctors share a passion for continuing education, keeping up with the latest advancements in modern dentistry to ensure our patients always receive the highest standard of care. More importantly, our team values the human connection behind every smile. We take the time to listen to your concerns, understand your goals, and craft customized treatment plans that respect your lifestyle and comfort levels.

Deeply Committed to the Buford Community

Buford is more than just where our office is located—it is our home. We are deeply committed to giving back to the Buford community through high-quality, compassionate dentistry and ongoing local engagement. For us, success is measured not only by the smiles we transform inside our practice, but also by the lasting, positive impact we make in the lives of our neighbors and friends.
