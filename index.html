About Buford Family Dental

Buford Family Dental is a premier, modern practice dedicated to providing comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages in Buford, Georgia. Guided by the expertise of Dr. Ravi Patel, Dr. Sun Kim, and Dr. Rohit Mukhi, our team combines state-of-the-art technology with a warm, patient-first approach. From routine cleanings to advanced restorative treatments, we take the time to keep you fully informed about your oral health, planned procedures, and costs every step of the way. With flexible financing options designed to fit diverse budgets, we make prioritizing your family’s smiles simple. Our Services

  • General & Preventive Dentistry: Routine cleanings, oral exams, digital X-rays, fluoride treatments, and custom nightguards to maintain optimal oral health.
  • Cosmetic Dentistry: Professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, and cosmetic bonding designed to enhance your natural smile.
  • Restorative Dentistry: Dental crowns, bridges, tooth-colored fillings, and dental implant restorations to rebuild function and aesthetics.
  • Pediatric Dentistry: Gentle, friendly dental care tailored specifically for young patients to establish positive habits early in life.
  • Periodontal Care: Comprehensive gum disease screening, deep cleanings, and maintenance treatments to protect your supporting bone and gums.
  • Emergency Dental Care: Prompt, attentive treatment for unexpected dental pain, broken teeth, or urgent oral injuries.
